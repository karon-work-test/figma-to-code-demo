What is a Migraine?

More Than Just a Headache

A migraine is a complex neurological condition characterized by recurring, often debilitating headache attacks. Episodes can last between 4 and 72 hours and are frequently accompanied by nausea, vomiting, and extreme sensitivity to light and sound.

Migraines affect over 1 billion people worldwide, making them the third most prevalent illness globally. Despite their prevalence, migraines are frequently under treated and misunderstood.
